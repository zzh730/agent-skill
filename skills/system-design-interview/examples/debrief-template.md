### L6 Signal Checklist (for L6 candidates)

- [ ] Proactively scoped requirements before designing
- [ ] Explicitly stated correctness invariants
- [ ] Reasoned about trade-offs with concrete alternatives
- [ ] Anticipated failure modes without prompting
- [ ] Addressed cross-system consistency (dual-write, outbox, etc.)
- [ ] Discussed observability: metrics, alerts, debug workflow
- [ ] Considered team boundaries and ownership
- [ ] Thought about evolution over time

## Tone Guidance

- Be honest but constructive
- Lead with strengths before gaps
- Use specific examples, not general statements
- Frame gaps as growth opportunities, not failures
- End with actionable next steps
